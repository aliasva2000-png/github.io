For New Students

Advice I
Wish I Had

01

Memorize less, understand more

The student who understands why the kidney reabsorbs sodium will never forget the renin-angiotensin system. Understanding outlasts memory every time.

02

Sleep is not a weakness

Medicine glorifies suffering through exhaustion. A rested mind catches things a tired one misses. Protect your sleep like you protect your patients.

03

Talk to patients, not at them

The history is 80% of the diagnosis. The patient will tell you what is wrong if you learn to ask and — more importantly — to listen without interrupting.

04

Build your identity early

Start writing, sharing, and building your name while you are still a student. Your future patients and colleagues will find you long before you graduate.
